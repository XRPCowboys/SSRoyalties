Client may license our software, configure custom payout split sheets, and monitor cleared royalties in real-time. 3. Redirection of Revenues & Virtual Account Clearing Rails

To activate your automated payout distributions, you must execute a legally binding Letter of Direction (LOD) or Payee Remittance Direction authorizing the redirection of your gross revenue payments from your current distributors, performance rights organizations, or operating clearing houses directly to the designated clearing coordinates configured in the software interface. Client-dedicated virtual bank clearing accounts (vIBANs) are provisioned programmatically through regulated Banking-as-a-Service (BaaS) infrastructure partners and clearing network providers across North America, the United Kingdom, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. The Company cannot initiate payout distributions until cleared funds are received at our gateway coordinates.

4. Payout Distribution Waterfalls & Fee Models

4.1 Master Sound Recording & Physical Asset Net Splits

The software tool provides a customizable split-sheet and automated waterfall system. For convenience, the software is pre-loaded with a standard, default template that licensees can choose to apply, allocating cleared master and physical revenues received at their secure coordinates and instantly routing them through native ledger primitives to secure wallets as follows:

  • The Owner / Rights Holder: 35% Net Split (Configurable default).
  • The B2B Partner (Operating/Recording Studio): 15% Net Split (Configurable default).
  • Split Second Royalties Software Licensing Cut: 38% Net Split (Standard default for utilizing our software and technology. This flat licensing fee supports the network infrastructure, hosting, and database maintenance on autopilot, and is billed directly at the ledger level).
  • The Client-Managed Project Support Pool: 12% Net Split (Configurable default. If the licensee chooses to raise capital from independent project supporters, the software enables them to programmatically route this split on-chain directly to active project supporter wallets, eliminating nested on-chain bifurcations and platform sweeps).

4.2 Composition (Publishing) Software Splits

For underlying musical work copyrights, the software tool includes a standard default template under which the Client may utilize the platform's white-label administration wizard to manage global registrations and generate automated registration files. Under this arrangement, the Company takes zero copyright ownership and acts solely as a technology provider (Option A White-Label Admin Utility), while the B2B Client (or their designated third-party administrator) acts as the actual legal and operational Publishing Administrator.

To accommodate music industry regulations where the songwriter's 50% Writer's Share of performance royalties is legally unassignable at the PRO level, the platform utilizes a Dual-Channel Settlement Rule to achieve the identical 38% SSR software licensing and 12% Project Support Pool economics:

  • Channel A: Performance Royalties (ASCAP/BMI/SESAC): Because the 50% Writer's Share is legally unassignable and paid directly to the creator by the PRO, the platform routes 100% of the incoming 50% Publisher's Share on-chain as: 76% to the SSR Software Licensing Fee (representing 38% of the total royalty) and 24% to the Project Support Pool (representing 12% of the total royalty).
  • Channel B: Mechanical Royalties (MLC/DSPs): Since 100% of gross mechanical royalties are routed directly to the platform's clearing coordinates under a standard administrative LOD, the software programmatically routes and splits these funds on-chain as: 38% to the SSR Software Licensing Fee, 50% directly to the Writer/Creator's secure wallet, and 12% to the Project Support Pool.

4.3 Single-Owner Corporate Portals & Enterprise Pricing Model

For high-volume B2B corporate entities (such as public mineral aggregators, patent holders, or corporate catalog owners) who directly own and control 100% of their royalty-producing assets and do not utilize public project supporter pools or multi-party split-sheets, the platform provides a Single-Owner Corporate Portal configuration. Under this configuration, standard multi-party waterfall templates are disabled. The ledger settlement engine programmatically applies the Enterprise Corporate Metered Model (5.55% unbundled total transaction routing rate, comprising a 5.00% net software licensing fee, a 0.50% operational infrastructure fee, and the standard 0.05% clearing pass-through fee). The remaining 94.45% of gross routed revenues are swept instantly and natively to the client's secure corporate MPC coordinates in seconds, with zero platform custody.

Year 1 Onboarding Floor Waiver & Year 2+ Commitment Floor:

  • Year 1 Onboarding Phase (Months 1–12): To accommodate initial corporate onboarding, well-batch testing, and portfolio migration, the annual platform commitment floor is 100% waived ($0 Annual Minimum). Client pays strictly the metered processing fee rate on live processed volume during Launch Year 1, allowing full flexibility to onboard property tranches at Client's own pace.
  • Year 2+ Standard Production Phase (Months 13+): Beginning in Month 13, live production accounts are subject to a $90,000 Annual Minimum Platform Commitment Floor. Client shall pay either their calculated marginal volume fee or the $90,000 platform floor—whichever is greater—guaranteeing baseline production continuity and cloud infrastructure reservation.
  • Dynamic Master Admin Parameters: All fee rates, volume brackets, and onboarding milestones are governed dynamically via the Master Admin Portal and individual Commercial Order Forms (Schedule A).

5. Technical Ingestion, Hashing, and Storage

White-Glove Setup: Upon receipt of your metadata and standard spreadsheets, our delivery team will encrypt your files with AES-256 and anchor their unique SHA-256 cryptographic hashes directly to the XRP Ledger within 48 hours to prove unalterable provenance of your legal rights.

Underwriting Filter: Under rolling software policies, the licensed software integrates an automated filter that screens out and excludes non-performing, under-threshold assets (specifically, music tracks averaging under 1,000 rolling annual streams) to protect project yields from digital platform monetization thresholds.

6. Active Wallet Operations & Transaction Fees

Client accounts are provisioned with secure, non-custodial Multi-Party Computation (MPC) wallets. Client retains full ownership and control over cleared royalties, with the ability to sweep funds, execute peer-to-peer transfers, and route custom splits. To cover regulated clearing rails, virtual account provisioning (vIBANs), and network execution across US, UK, EU, and APAC jurisdictions, the platform enforces a flat transaction pass-through fee of 5 basis points (0.05%), which is passed through directly to underlying infrastructure partners without platform markup. Split Second Royalties receives zero mark-up or transaction-based margins.

7. Account Termination and Offboarding

You may terminate your account at any time upon 30 days' written notice. Upon termination, you must submit a counter-LOD to your distributors to redirect your cash flows back to your personal coordinates. Within 30 days of termination, we will provide you with all decrypted files and historical transaction logs in a simple, standardized text format, programmatically close your sub-tenancy portals, and deactivate your split sheets.

8. Automated Legal Document & SEC Compliance Processing

Tier A Corporate SAFE Automation: For the Company's own corporate-level seed round, the system programmatically generates a Simple Agreement for Future Equity (SAFE) drawn from the 1,000,000-share Strategic Corporate Reserve, pre-set at a $45,000,000 valuation cap, and routes it directly to the seed investor via a secure white-labeled e-signing API.

Tier B Project PPM & Subscription Automation: For licensees who choose to secure project supporters, the portal programmatically compiles customized, asset-specific Private Placement Memorandums (PPMs) and Subscription Agreements, dynamically injecting specific asset identifiers (such as ISRCs, ISWCs, or mineral tract numbers) and localized risk factors prior to on-chain split execution, while restricting supporter wallet activation until execution.

Automated Form D Reporting: On the exact Date of First Sale of any offering pool, the software tool automatically compiles all required SEC Form D metadata and exports a pre-populated Form D Draft Worksheet (PDF/XML) for the licensee to safeguard the 15-calendar-day SEC filing deadline and prevent EDGAR portal session timeouts.

On-Chain Compliance Screening: The platform's compliance middleware enforces integrated identity verification (KYC/KYB) and real-time screening against the U.S. OFAC Specially Designated Nationals registry, programmatically blocking wallet provisioning or payout routing for any flagged entities.
